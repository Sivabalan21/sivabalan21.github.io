## Problem

Choosing electives at university involves a lot of moving parts: students have to be onboarded, faculty assigned to courses, and seats allocated fairly under capacity limits. We set out to build one portal that handled all three and stayed fast under load.

## What we built

A high-performance portal for:

- **Elective onboarding**, so students could register and pick their electives
- **Faculty assignment** to courses
- **Optimized allocation** of students to electives

The backend was **Node.js and Express** on **MySQL**, hosted on **AWS RDS**, with schema design as a core part of the work. Tests were written with **Jest**.

## My role

The team had five people, and I wore three hats:

- **Full stack developer.** I led backend development and developed 5 of the key features.
- **DevOps engineer.** I deployed the system on **GCP** with **Docker**, and set up **CI/CD with Jenkins and GitLab**.
- **Scrum Master.** I ran sprint planning and stand-ups in **Jira**, and mentored teammates.

I also presented the project.

Looking back, this is where software engineering started for me as a practice, not just coursework: shared code, deployments, pipelines, and a team to coordinate, before my first job.
